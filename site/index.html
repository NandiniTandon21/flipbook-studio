01 — Why this exists

Try finding a free flipbook maker that just… lets you have your flipbook.

I wanted to turn a few PDFs into a magazine that flips. Simple enough. Except every “free” flipbook maker I found wanted something first: sign up, start a trial, upgrade to remove the watermark, pay to download. Most wouldn’t let you download the flipbook at all. It lives on their site, behind their login, for as long as they feel like hosting it.

I couldn’t find a single one that was free, didn’t make me sign up, and let me keep the result. So I made it myself.

Flipbook Studio is one file you download and open. It runs entirely on your computer and never uploads anything. The flipbook you make is yours: a single file you can keep, send, or open years from now without anyone’s permission.

Made for me, and for anyone else who’s been on the same hunt.

02 — How it works
  1. 01

    Download & open

    Download one file and double-click it. It opens in your browser and works offline. Nothing to install, no account.

  2. 02

    Add & arrange

    Drop in PDFs and images. Every PDF page becomes a magazine page. Drag them into order: the cover first, then spreads.

  3. 03

    Read, save, download

    Flip through it, save it to your shelf, and download the flipbook: one file that opens in any browser, anywhere.

03 — What you get
Real page turns
Two-page spreads with a horizontal 3D page flip. Click, swipe, use the arrow keys, or go full screen.
A flipbook you keep
Download it as one .html file that opens offline in any browser. Share it, archive it, or drop it back in to edit.
Searchable text
Text inside PDFs stays selectable and searchable. Text in images and scans is read offline (English, for now).
PDFs, photos, HEIC
PDF, JPG, PNG, WebP, and iPhone HEIC photos, in every browser.
Your shelf
Save flipbooks in your browser to read, edit, or download later. Unsaved work is never lost silently.
Private by design
The app is built so it can’t reach the internet. No uploads, no tracking, no cookies. That goes for this page too.
04 — Questions

Things people ask.

Is it actually free?

Yes. No trial, no premium tier, no watermark. The code is open source under the MIT licence.

Do my files get uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens inside your browser on your computer. The app carries a browser security policy that blocks every network request, so it couldn’t upload even if it tried.

Do I need the internet?

Only to download it once. After that it works fully offline, and so do the flipbooks you make.

What do I get when I download a flipbook?

A single .html file containing your pages and the page-turning reader. Double-click it and it opens in any modern browser, on any computer, with nothing else needed.

Which browsers and computers?

Chrome, Edge, Safari, and Firefox on Mac, Windows, and Linux. Every release is tested in all three browser engines.

Can it read text in other languages?

Text already inside a PDF works in any language. Reading text from images (OCR) is English-only for now. More languages are on the roadmap.

Where are my saved flipbooks stored?

In your browser’s storage on that computer. The files you download are your permanent copies, so keep those somewhere safe.
